The Anatomy of an Heirloom.

In our Lagos and partner London workshops, there are no shortcuts. We refuse composite leathers and synthetic bonding. Every hide is inspected for tensile fiber integrity, hand-skived to fractional millimeter tolerances, and sewn with double-waxed braided threads.
